# Working Book Project

**Author:** V.T.R. Ravi Kumar  
**Working repository name:** `vtr-venba`  
**Final book title:** Undecided  
**Status:** Architecture approved; manuscript development not yet started

## Purpose

This repository holds the manuscript and editorial documentation for an original work of English verse shaped by the formal discipline of traditional Tamil **குறள் வெண்பா (Kural Venba)**. It is not a translation of the Thirukkural, nor should it be presented as one.

The work will explore human thought and experience across the modern period, with a broad horizon from 1900 to 2027. Its aim is to make the concerns of each chapter recognisable to readers with different lives, choices, relationships, and beliefs.

## Fixed architecture

- **9 Parts**
- **72 Chapters** (8 per Part)
- **720 kurals** (10 per Chapter)

The first eight Parts follow a conceptual progression through eight-year life stages, from ages 0–8 through 57–64. Part IX moves beyond age-based stages into bliss, detachment, death, the unknown, and ultimate truth. These stages are a philosophical organising device, not a claim that every life follows the same path.

The approved chapter list and stage framework are documented in [the architecture](docs/architecture.md).

## Editorial commitments

- Keep every Chapter's central territory distinct; avoid repeating the same idea under different names.
- Make the work inclusive of varied life paths; marriage, parenthood, conventional careers, and any single worldview must not be assumed.
- Do not draft the kurals until the chapter territories and verse-level topic tracking are in place.
- Research and document how English pronunciation can be evaluated against Tamil prosodic concepts. Do not assume a direct one-to-one mapping.
- Validate each verse against the relevant Kural Venba rules, including asai, seer, thalai, line structure, and ending requirements, with specialist review where needed.
- Keep claims about formal compliance honest: unverified examples must never be labelled as metrically validated.

## Repository layout

- `docs/architecture.md` — approved Parts and Chapter structure.
- `docs/editorial-principles.md` — thematic, linguistic, and validation principles.
- `manuscript/front-matter/` — title and preliminary pages, developed later.
- `manuscript/parts/` — manuscript content organised by Part.

## Publishing

The manuscript is intended to follow the author's VTR Press workflow and conventions. PDF is the primary planned output; EPUB is optional. This repository contains book source material and editorial records, not the VTR Press publishing engine.

2. Establish a chapter-by-chapter topic register before composing verses.
3. Research and document a defensible English-to-Tamil prosody validation method.
4. Develop the front matter and Parts incrementally.
5. Draft and validate kurals only after the editorial groundwork is ready.
