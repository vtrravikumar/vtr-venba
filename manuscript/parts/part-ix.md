# Part IX — Bliss, Death and Ultimate Truth

**Status:** Initial composition workshop — all verse candidates remain drafts until pair-level editorial review and formal validation.

Part IX is being drafted first because it provides the philosophical perspective through which the preceding eight Parts can later be revisited. It explores spirituality, belief, detachment, mortality, and the limits of what can be known. Detachment must not be confused with indifference: people may profoundly shape our lives without being ours to possess or constituting the entirety of our journey.

## Chapter 65 — The Freedom Beyond Want

**Chapter purpose:** Explore the freedom that emerges when we stop treating people, possessions, achievements, or experiences as things we must permanently own or control.

### Pair 65.1 — Companions, not possessions

**Shared insight:** People may accompany and transform our journey, but love does not make them our possessions.

**Tamil candidate**

உறவுகள் வாழ்வின் வழித்துணை ஆவர்  
உடைமையென் றெண்ணின் துயர்

**English companion candidate**

Those who walk beside us shape our days;  
No soul is ours to keep against the tide.

**Status and review notes**
- Concept draft; not approved.
- The Tamil wording has not yet been fully scanned for அசை, சீர், தளை, செப்பலோசை, and the required ஈற்றுச்சீர். Its two-line presentation and apparent lexical grouping do not establish Kural Venba validity.
- The English companion is an independent literary expression of the shared insight. Its final formal method remains to be defined.
- Review for naturalness, philosophical precision, distinctiveness, and whether the image of the tide adds meaning rather than decoration.
