# Front and Back Matter

The active front- and back-matter draft is [../front-matter.md](../front-matter.md). It follows the YAML metadata and section-heading conventions demonstrated by the VTR Press sample manuscript.

## Included in the draft

- YAML book metadata, with the final title and subtitle left as placeholders.
- Copyright page, including publication details that still need confirmation.
- Dedication placeholder.
- Prologue draft explaining the book's structure, bilingual literary approach, and prosodic commitments.
- Epilogue placeholder to be completed after the full manuscript is written.
- Author biography draft and finalisation placeholder.

## Contents and production rules

- **Do not insert a manually maintained table of contents.** The contents should be generated by VTR Press from the final Part and Chapter structure, then verified in the rendered PDF. Check EPUB output too if it is produced.
- Keep front and back matter in the same source manuscript as the Parts when preparing the final production input, unless the VTR Press workflow explicitly supports and requires a different assembly convention.
- Replace every visible placeholder before publication.
- Confirm the final title, subtitle, edition, copyright year, ISBN (if applicable), publisher/imprint, place of publication, dedication, author biography, and any public links before release.
- The book treats Tamil and English as equal literary partners. Any explanation of metrical form must distinguish traditional Tamil rules from the separately documented approach used for English compositions. Do not claim formal compliance until the method and verses have been reviewed.
